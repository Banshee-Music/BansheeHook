# BansheeHook
Fair-launch and liquidity hook built on top of Uniswap V4.

Inspired by the Flaunch launchpad model, Banshee enables artists to launch ERC-1155 songs, albums, and live-performance tickets through a controlled fair-launch period before transitioning into an open liquidity market.

The Hook uses time-weighted market activation to reduce launch-time order-flow shocks and improve price discovery, while trading fees can support artist royalties and sustainable LP incentives. As part of the broader Banshee platform, Proof-of-Performance can also be verified by the Banshee AI Agent using music, performance, and indexed market data.
